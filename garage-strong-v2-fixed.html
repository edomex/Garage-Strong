Garage Strong 2.0 plan

Four 30–40 minute sessions. Back-to-back exercise pairs, then rest about 60–90 seconds; longer after heavy compounds as needed. Farmer's carries finish every day.

Equipment & weight calculations

For the original barbell exercises, enter plate weight only; Garage Strong adds your saved bar weight. For dumbbells, log weight of ONE complete dumbbell. For farmers' carries, plate and handle weights are stored separately.

A blank bar weight means no automatic addition. Saved history is never recalculated.
